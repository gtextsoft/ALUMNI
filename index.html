Welcome to the Gtext Alumni Community

The Gtext Alumni Community connects former Gtext employees, interns, associates, and team members across different locations and industries.

Whether you want to reconnect with old colleagues, explore business opportunities, share your expertise, or stay connected to the Gtext family, this community is for you.

Who the community is for

Anyone who worked with Gtext can join, whatever the role and however long you stayed.

That includes full-time staff, part-time colleagues, interns, NYSC corps members, contractors, and consultants. Some alumni are still with Gtext. Others have moved into new jobs, businesses, and cities.

The community reaches across the Gtext family.

  • Gtext Homes
  • Gtext Land
  • Gtext & Associates
  • Gtext Farms
  • Gtext Holdings
  • Gtext Suites

Alumni register from Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, other parts of Nigeria, and outside Nigeria.

What you can get from the community

Tell us what you want when you register. These are the reasons alumni come back.

Networking
Find old colleagues and meet alumni you never worked beside.
Career opportunities
Hear about roles and introductions from people who know your work.
Business opportunities
Share what you are building and find alumni who want to work with you.
Mentorship
Ask someone who has already walked the path you are on.
Events and reunions
Get word when the community gathers, online or in person.
Learning and knowledge sharing
Pass on what you learned at Gtext, and pick up what others have learned since.
Collaboration
Start something with people who already understand how Gtext works.
Giving back
Mentor someone newer than you, and keep the door open for the next group.

How you join

  1. Register

    Fill in the short form. Only your name, contact, location, Gtext entity, role, and years are required.

  2. Join the WhatsApp community

    After you submit, you get the link to the official Gtext Alumni WhatsApp Community for updates and events.

  3. Stay reachable

    Save the Gtext Alumni contact and turn on notifications so you do not miss what comes next.
